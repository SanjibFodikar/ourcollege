Admission Process

Follow these simple steps to complete your admission successfully.

Step 1
Submit Application

Fill out the online admission form with accurate personal and academic details.

Step 2
Document Verification

Upload or submit all required academic certificates and supporting documents.

Step 3
Interview / Assessment

Eligible applicants may be invited for an interaction, entrance test, or assessment.

Eligibility & Required Documents

Applicants must meet the academic eligibility criteria and provide all required documentation during the admission process.

Academic Qualification

Minimum required marks in previous qualifying examinations as per program guidelines.

Identity Proof

Valid government-issued ID or birth certificate for verification purposes.

Academic Documents

Mark sheets, transfer certificate, character certificate, and passport-size photographs.
